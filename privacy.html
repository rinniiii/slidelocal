Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Slidelocal runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by its owner.

  • Data from TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar) and access tokens of the owner's own TikTok account, used only to send drafts to that account.
  • Storage: tokens are stored locally in the computer's encrypted keychain. Nothing is stored on any server.
  • Images: slideshow images are placed on this website only for the few minutes TikTok needs to download them, then deleted.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Deletion: disconnecting the account in the tool, or revoking access in TikTok settings, removes the tokens.
